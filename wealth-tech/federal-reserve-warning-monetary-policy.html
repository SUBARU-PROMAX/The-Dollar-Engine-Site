Historical Context

To understand the current state of monetary policy, it is essential to examine the historical context. The Federal Reserve was established in 1913, with the primary objective of promoting maximum employment, stable prices, and moderate long-term interest rates. Over the years, the Fed has used various tools, including open market operations, reserve requirements, and interest rates, to achieve its objectives. However, the Fed's actions have not always been successful, and the economy has experienced several periods of boom and bust.
