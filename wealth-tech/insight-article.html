The 'status quo personalization' of 2024—which mostly consisted of basic product recommendations based on past purchases—has been replaced by 'Anticipatory Banking.' This paradigm uses financial data as a fluid ecosystem to predict account holder needs before they are even communicated. This is achieved through three distinct pillars:

    • Predictive: Using deep-learning analytics to forecast cash flow gaps or life events (e.g., a child entering college) six to twelve months in advance.
